How these tiers are defined
Restorative
Demonstrates net-positive contribution to kelp forest recovery, plus a solid baseline across locality, transparency, and labor criteria. Existing sustainable-seafood rating systems have no equivalent tier — they score harm avoidance, not restoration.
Sustainable
Low-harm and compliant, but not demonstrating documented net-positive restoration contribution.
Developing
Meaningful gaps in data or practice. This is a path, not a rejection — organizations here are working toward disclosure or practice changes.

Carbon impact is deliberately not scored numerically. Kelp carbon sequestration remains genuinely unsettled science, and a clean number would imply more rigor than currently exists.
